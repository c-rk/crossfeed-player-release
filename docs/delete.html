delete your crossfeed account

Crossfeed and Crossfeed Player · dev.crossfeed, dev.crossfeed.player

Crossfeed accounts are a handle and nothing else. No email address, no password, no real name. There are two ways to get rid of one.

if you still have the app

Open auxshare, scroll to the bottom, and tap wipe my account.

It happens immediately and cannot be undone.

if you no longer have the app

Email murdawk@hypixonic.com with the subject Crossfeed account deletion and tell us the handle you used.

Requests are actioned within seven days. You will get a reply confirming it is done.

what gets deleted

  • Your handle, and the account it belongs to
  • Every song you posted
  • Every reaction you gave, and every reaction anyone gave to your posts
  • Your saved tracks
  • Every connection between you and anyone else

Nothing is retained afterwards. There is no archive, no backup copy and no soft delete.

what was never on the server

Your listening history and your music library only ever existed on your phone. Uninstalling the app removes them, and deleting your account has no bearing on them either way.

Handles are permanent. Deleting an account frees the handle, but the key to it lived only on that phone. If you delete and later want the same handle back, you have to ask for it and have it granted by hand.
